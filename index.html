Switch Telemetry & Key Chatter Detector

Failing mechanical or scissor switches often suffer from key chatter (switch bounce)—registering double or triple presses from a single actuation. Our sub-millisecond timer measures hold dwell duration and flags any key that re-triggers within <15ms.

How do I test my Mac or Windows keyboard keys online?

Simply press any physical key on your keyboard while on this page. The corresponding keycap lights up in bright system blue while held down and turns emerald green once verified working.

What is Key Chatter (Switch Bounce) and how does the detector work?

Key chatter occurs when dust, oxidation, or leaf fatigue inside a mechanical or scissor switch causes a single press to register multiple times rapidly. Our telemetry engine tracks the timestamp between key release and actuation—highlighting the key in amber if a bounce occurs in under 15ms.
